Incident Report: rserver3.exe Detection via Custom IOA Policy

## Executive Summary

On **June 23, 2026 at 08:12:04**, CrowdStrike Falcon generated a **Custom Indicator of Attack (IOA)** alert involving the execution of **rserver3.exe** on endpoint **5CG2490TM9**.

Investigation confirmed that the executable was associated with **Radmin Server**, a legitimate remote administration tool. While no malicious activity or compromise was identified, the application violated organizational security policy prohibiting unauthorized remote administration software. The detection triggered as intended through an established CrowdStrike custom IOA rule, and the incident was classified as a **True Positive**.

### Justification

Although the executable is a legitimate application, it provides remote administration capabilities that are not approved within the organization's environment. Unauthorized remote access tools can increase risk by creating unmanaged access paths and may be leveraged by threat actors if improperly configured or abused. As a result, the activity constitutes a policy violation and warrants response despite the absence of malicious intent.

---

## Resolution

The alert was investigated and validated using CrowdStrike telemetry, process execution data, and reputation analysis.

Analysis confirmed that the detection was generated by a custom CrowdStrike IOA rule specifically designed to identify and prevent the use of Radmin Server. No evidence of malware execution, unauthorized access, or endpoint compromise was identified during the investigation.

In accordance with organizational policy, the service and executable were blocked, and no further malicious activity was observed.

---

## Lessons Learned

- Legitimate software can still represent a security risk when it violates organizational policy.
- Custom IOA rules provide valuable visibility and enforcement for unauthorized administrative tools.
- Remote administration utilities should be reviewed, approved

## Final Disposition

> **Closed – True Positive (Unauthorized Remote Administration Tool Detected and Blocked)**

### Conclusion

CrowdStrike Falcon generated a Custom IOA alert after detecting the execution of **rserver3.exe** on endpoint **5CG2490TM9**. Investigation determined that the executable was associated with **Radmin Server**, a legitimate remote administration application. Although no evidence of malware, endpoint compromise, privilege escalation, or unauthorized activity was identified, the software violated organizational policy prohibiting unauthorized remote access tools.

The CrowdStrike custom IOA rule successfully detected and blocked the application as designed. Review of process execution, file reputation, and endpoint activity revealed no additional malicious indicators. The remote administration service was blocked in accordance with security policy, and no further remediation actions were required.

Based on the investigation findings, the event was classified as a **True Positive policy violation**, successfully contained by existing security controls, and the incident was closed.
